# ASEF privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

ASEF is a personal money tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

## What ASEF stores

The income, expenses, accounts, transfers, budgets, repeating entries, assets, debts, side hustles and
savings goals you type into the app, and your settings (main currency and exchange rates).

## Your account

To use ASEF you create an account with your email address, a password and, if you want, your name. The
account is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google, which stores your email, your name, a
protected form of your password and the dates you signed up and last logged in. ASEF uses it only to let you
log in, reset your password, confirm your email and delete your account. Google's privacy policy applies to
that service: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Where your money data is stored

Only on your phone, in the app's own storage, kept separately for each account that logs in on the phone. Your
income, expenses, accounts, assets and every other amount you type are never sent to us, to Firebase or to
anyone else.

## Live prices

If **Live prices** is on (you can turn it off in Settings), ASEF downloads public exchange rates from
open.er-api.com, crypto and gold prices from CoinGecko and stock prices from Yahoo Finance. The only thing
sent is the coin or stock symbol being looked up (for example BTC or AAPL), never amounts, names or any other
data you entered. Like any web request, these services see your IP address.

## Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled on your phone by ASEF itself. Nothing goes through a server.

## What ASEF does not do

- No analytics, tracking or advertising.
- No collection of your contacts, location or device identifiers. Your email and name are used only for
  your account.
- No access to your bank: everything is typed by you.

## Backups and exports

When you choose **Save backup** or **Export CSV**, ASEF creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet.
You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, WhatsApp or email). Once shared, that file is handled
by the app or service you picked, under its own privacy policy. **Restore backup** reads a file you pick.

## Deleting your account and data

In ASEF, **Settings → Delete account** deletes your account from Firebase and everything ASEF keeps for it on
the phone. Uninstalling ASEF deletes everything it stored on the phone, but not the account itself. You can
also ask for your account to be deleted by opening an issue at the address below. Backup files you saved
elsewhere stay where you put them.

## Children

ASEF is not directed at children under 13.
