# ASEF privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

ASEF is a personal money tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

## What ASEF stores

The income, expenses, accounts, transfers, budgets, repeating entries, assets, debts, side hustles and
savings goals you type into the app, and your settings (main currency and exchange rates).

## Where it is stored

Only on your phone, in the app's own storage. ASEF has no account, no server and no sign-in. Your data is
never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Live prices

If **Live prices** is on (you can turn it off in Settings), ASEF downloads public exchange rates from
open.er-api.com, crypto and gold prices from CoinGecko and stock prices from Yahoo Finance. The only thing
sent is the coin or stock symbol being looked up (for example BTC or AAPL), never amounts, names or any other
data you entered. Like any web request, these services see your IP address.

## Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled on your phone by ASEF itself. Nothing goes through a server.

## What ASEF does not do

- No analytics, tracking or advertising.
- No collection of your name, email, contacts, location or device identifiers.
- No access to your bank: everything is typed by you.

## Backups and exports

When you choose **Save backup** or **Export CSV**, ASEF creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet.
You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, WhatsApp or email). Once shared, that file is handled
by the app or service you picked, under its own privacy policy. **Restore backup** reads a file you pick.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling ASEF deletes everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

## Children

ASEF is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
