## Full description
ASEF helps you see where your money goes every day, and grow what you keep.

TRACK EVERY DIRHAM
• Add income or an expense in a few taps
• Moroccan dirham, euro and US dollar, with one total in the currency you choose
• Cash, bank, wallet and savings accounts, and transfers between them
• History by day, week, month or all time

PLAN YOUR MONTH
• Monthly budgets per category, with what's left or over
• Repeating income and bills (salary, rent, subscriptions) added on their day
• Six months of income and expenses at a glance

BUILD WEALTH
• Net worth: your cash, plus everything you own, minus what you owe
• Property, land, gold, stocks, crypto, savings, vehicles and businesses, each with its value history
• See each asset's gain, the rent or dividends it earns and its yield
• Debts with how much is paid off
• Side hustles: what each one really earns after its costs
• Savings goals with how much to put aside each month

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays on your phone
• Back up to Drive, WhatsApp or email and restore on a new phone
• Export to CSV for any spreadsheet
